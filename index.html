Personal SMS Safety Alerts

Benjamin Kruse Personal Safety Alerts is a personal, non-commercial text message alert program operated by Benjamin Kruse, an individual (sole proprietor) in Phoenix, Arizona. It sends automated safety alerts by SMS from the toll-free number (855) 450-1642.

What alerts are sent

  • Weather and storm alerts – severe weather, storms, heat, and rain affecting the Phoenix area, based on public National Weather Service (NWS) information.
  • Road closure alerts – closures and incidents on local routes, based on public Arizona Department of Transportation AZ511 information.
  • Motorcycle ride warnings – notices when weather or road conditions make a planned motorcycle ride unsafe.

Who receives them

The only recipient is the program owner, Benjamin Kruse, on his own mobile phone. He opted in himself using the opt-in form. The program is not offered to the public and has no other subscribers.

What is not sent

No marketing, promotional, or advertising messages are ever sent. No products or services are sold.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies with conditions, about 10 messages per month. Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help at any time.

Example message

Benjamin Kruse Safety Alerts: NWS storm warning for Phoenix until 7:00 PM. Avoid riding your motorcycle until it passes. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help.
