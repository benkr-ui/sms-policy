Privacy Policy

This policy covers Benjamin Kruse Personal Safety Alerts, operated by Benjamin Kruse (Phoenix, Arizona), which sends SMS safety alerts from (855) 450-1642.

Information collected

The only information collected is the mobile phone number of the single recipient (the program owner, Benjamin Kruse) and the record of his opt-in consent. No other personal data is collected.

How it is used

The mobile number is used only to send the safety alerts described on the Home page and to respond to STOP and HELP requests.

No sharing or selling

Mobile numbers and SMS opt-in data and consent are never shared with, sold to, or rented to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. Information is shared only with the SMS delivery provider (Twilio) as required to deliver messages.

Storage and security

The number is stored in the owner's messaging provider account, protected by a password and two-step sign-in. It is kept while the program runs and deleted when the program ends or after STOP is sent.

Opting out

Reply STOP at any time to stop messages. Reply HELP for help. See the SMS Terms.
