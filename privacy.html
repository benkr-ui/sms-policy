Privacy Policy

Benjamin Kruse personal safety alerts

This program sends SMS text messages from the toll-free number (855) 450-1642 only to the account owner's own mobile phone. Messages are personal safety alerts: weather, storm, road closure, and motorcycle ride safety warnings.

No marketing or promotional messages are sent. The phone number receiving alerts is never sold, rented, or shared with any third party for any purpose. Mobile opt-in data and consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates.

How your number is collected, stored, and protected

The only phone number in this program is the account owner's own, entered by the owner. It is stored only in the owner's Twilio account, which is protected by a password and two-step sign-in, and is used only to send these alerts. It is kept while the program runs and is deleted when the program ends or when STOP is sent.

See also the SMS Terms.

Message frequency varies (approx. up to 10 per month). Message and data rates may apply.
